Managing fecal incontinence is challenging for patients in the intensive care unit (ICU) and the healthcare staff who care for them. Traditional fecal management solutions involve the use of absorbent pads and intrarectal balloon catheters. While these methods help to manage fecal effluents, these are associated with various clinical complications.
